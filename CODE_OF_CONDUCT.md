# Pulseq Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Pulseq community is committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, constructive, and harassment-free environment for all participants.

We welcome contributors from different backgrounds, career stages, institutions, countries, disciplines, and technical communities, including MRI physics, engineering, software development, clinical imaging, research, education, and open-source software.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- use welcoming and inclusive language
- respect differing viewpoints and experiences
- give and receive constructive technical feedback
- focus critique on ideas, code, documentation, data, and processes rather than individuals
- be respectful of contributors’ time and expertise
- help maintain a collaborative scientific and software-development environment

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- personal attacks or insulting comments
- sustained disruption of discussions, pull requests, issues, meetings, or community events
- publishing others’ private information without permission
- inappropriate or unwelcome conduct in public or private community spaces

## Enforcement

Concerns may be reported to the Pulseq organization owners or designated maintainers.

Reports should be handled respectfully, confidentially where appropriate, and with attention to the safety and dignity of affected community members.

Possible responses may include clarification, moderation, warning, temporary restriction, or removal from Pulseq community spaces.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to Pulseq GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, meetings, workshops, hackathons, and other community spaces associated with the Pulseq organization.
